Admissions

The Computer Science Department welcomes motivated students who are passionate about technology, innovation, and problem solving.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Intermediate with Mathematics or equivalent
  • Minimum required percentage as per university policy
  • Passing university entry test or interview

Application Process

  1. Fill out the online admission form
  2. Submit required academic documents
  3. Appear in entry test/interview
  4. Check merit list and confirm admission

Degree Programs

  • BS Computer Science
  • BS Software Engineering
  • BS Data Science
  • MS Computer Science

For detailed admission schedules and deadlines, please visit the university portal.
