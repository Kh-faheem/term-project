Admissions

The Computer Science Department welcomes motivated students who are passionate about technology, innovation, and problem solving. Join us to embark on an exciting journey in the world of computing and shape the future of technology.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Intermediate with Mathematics (Pre-Engineering/ICS) or equivalent qualification
  • Minimum 60% marks in intermediate or as per university admission policy
  • Valid SAT/NAT/University Entry Test score
  • Clear university entry test or interview process

Application Process

  1. Online Registration – Visit the university portal and create an account
  2. Fill Application Form – Complete the online admission form with accurate information
  3. Submit Documents – Upload scanned copies of required academic documents
  4. Entry Test/Interview – Appear in the entry test or scheduled interview
  5. Merit List – Check the merit list announcement on the official website
  6. Fee Submission – Pay the admission fee within the given deadline
  7. Confirm Admission – Complete the enrollment process and receive your student ID

Required Documents

  • SSC/Matriculation certificate and mark sheet
  • HSSC/Intermediate certificate and mark sheet
  • CNIC/B-Form copy
  • Domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Test score card (SAT/NAT/Entry Test)

Degree Programs Offered

  • BS Computer Science – 4-year undergraduate program (8 semesters)
  • BS Software Engineering – 4-year undergraduate program (8 semesters)
  • BS Data Science – 4-year undergraduate program (8 semesters)
  • BS Artificial Intelligence – 4-year undergraduate program (8 semesters)
  • MS Computer Science – 2-year graduate program (4 semesters)
  • PhD Computer Science – Research-based doctoral program

Important Dates

Spring Admissions: January - February
Fall Admissions: August - September
For detailed admission schedules, deadlines, and fee structure, please visit the official university admission portal or contact our admissions office.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Merit-based scholarships, need-based financial assistance, and fee concessions are available for deserving students. Contact the financial aid office for more details.
